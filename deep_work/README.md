# Deep Work

> 书中的一些摘要可以查看 [original.md](./original.md)

这本 *Deep Work* 当中写了很多故事， 逻辑严密的论证和原创性的东西比较少， 但是它确实把 Deep Work 这件事情本身拉到生活的中心位置来.

首先先探讨一下 `Deep Work` 的概念以及相对应的 `Shallow Work` 的概念

书中是这样阐述的:

**Deep Work**: Professional activities performed in a state of distraction-free concentration that push your cognitive capabilities to their limit. These efforts create new value, improve your skill, and are hard to replicate.

**Shallow Work**: Noncognitively demanding, logistical-style tasks, often performed while distracted. These efforts tend to not create much new value in the world and are easy to replicate.

书中还提到这样一种方法来进行区分:

How long would it take (in mouths) to train a smart recent college graduate with no specialized training in my field to complete this task ?

以上是这本书提到的基本概念， 但是我们这里的目的不是厘清整本书的脉络， 而是将这本书的相关内容进行进一步的探索和实践化， 我只会针对一些我认为有所启发的点进行说明和探索。
