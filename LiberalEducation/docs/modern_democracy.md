# Modern Democracy

This quote expresses a central concept in political theory known as **Elite Theory** (most famously associated with sociologists like Vilfredo Pareto, Gaetano Mosca, and Robert Michels).  
Here is a breakdown of what the sentence means in plain English:

### **Core Meaning**

> 1. **The Illusion of "Mass Rule"**: Democracy is often defined as "rule by the people" (the mass public). However, the author argues that true rule by the entire population is a practical impossibility—the masses simply cannot govern a modern society directly.  
> 2. **The Reality of Elite Governance**: Because the mass public cannot rule, actual power in a democracy always ends up in the hands of a small minority—the **elites**.  
> 3. **Who the Elites Are**: The author defines "elites" not just as a hereditary nobility or an exclusive aristocracy, but as **any organized group of people who are currently at the top of society, or who have the skills, influence, and opportunity to reach the top.**

### **Sentence Breakdown**

> * **"Modern democracy, so far from being universal aristocracy..."**  
>
  * *Meaning:* Modern democracy is definitely not a system where everyone is an aristocrat (where power/privilege is equal or refined across all citizens).  
>
> * **"...would be mass rule were it not for the fact that the mass cannot rule..."**  
>
  * *Meaning:* It *would* be pure rule by the ordinary majority, except for one critical problem: the general public is too large and unorganized to actually govern.  
>
> * **"...but is ruled by elites, that is, groupings of men who for whatever reason are on top or have a fair chance to arrive at the top;"**  
>
  * *Meaning:* Therefore, modern democracy is actually governed by elite groups—people who have successfully competed for power, influence, wealth, or political office, or who possess the realistic capacity to get there.

### **Key Takeaway**

The sentence argues that **even in a modern democracy, power is never truly held by "the people."** Instead, democracy is a political system where different elite groups compete for the right to rule over the majority.
